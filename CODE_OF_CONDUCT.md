# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, inclusive and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards others

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling or insulting comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the leadership.
